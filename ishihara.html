Read the number hidden in each circle of dots. 8 plates, about 2 minutes.

Before you start
  • Set screen brightness to high (~80%) and turn off Night Shift / f.lux / blue-light / tint filters.
  • Sit in normal room light, at comfortable arm's length, without tinted glasses.
  • Type exactly what you see. Some plates hold two digits, and you might see only one. If you see nothing, press "I can't see a number".
